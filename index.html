Conduct your whole rig from the SP-404MKII

Play, sequence and tweak your synths straight from the SP. Pads play notes, CTRL dials send CC, and patterns drive every synth on its own channel. No computer needed.

Free and open source. For Roland firmware 5.52, with DOOM OS 0.6.2 built in.

Everything your synths need, on the SP

DOOM OS turns the SP into a deeper sampler. MIDI Conductor turns it into the centre of your setup.

Sequence your synths

Every step of a pattern goes out as a MIDI note. Point pads at different synths and one pattern plays them all.

  • Each pad on its own synth or channel
  • Piano-roll pitches come with each step
  • Notes follow the step's gate

36 dials of CC

Turn on MIDI Controls and the CTRL dials send CC instead of moving the effect. Six banks of six, each dial set to any control on any synth.

  • SHIFT + FX1..FX6 picks the bank
  • MARK sets a dial's synth and control

Record dial moves

Sweep a filter while the pattern plays and it comes back on every loop. Each pattern keeps its own moves, saved on the SP.

  • SHIFT + VALUE starts recording
  • 64 points per dial for smooth sweeps
  • Grab a dial to take over live

Speaks your synths' language

Choose your synths when you patch and the SP knows their controls by name. Pick CUTOFF, not CC 43.

  • Korg Minilogue, NTS-1 and volcas
  • Behringer Grind and TD-3-MO, Roland S-1
  • Elektron Digitone II, Audiothingies MicroMonsta 2
  • Or load your own list

Route synth to synth

Play one synth's keys and another plays along. Map one synth's dials onto another's controls. The SP sits in the middle, so a single keyboard can drive the whole rig.

  • Notes, CC, pitch bend and aftertouch, each on or off per route
  • Turn a dial to map it

Set up on the unit

SHIFT + REMAIN opens MIDI SETTINGS: your synths, your pads, your routes. Every screen has a help line, and everything is saved.

Up and running

From Roland's download to a synth playing from a pad.

  1. Get Roland's firmware

    Download the SP-404MKII system update 5.52 from Roland and unzip it.

  2. Patch it

    Drop SP404MKII_APP1.bin on the patcher and pick your synths. Nothing is uploaded.

  3. Install it

    Install it with Roland's SP404MKII_APP0.bin, the same way as DOOM OS. DOOM OS is already inside.

  4. Play

    The quick start puts a synth on a pad and a dial in five steps.

Experimental. Modified firmware can brick your SP-404MKII. This is tested on one unit only, with Roland 5.52 and DOOM OS 0.6.2-alpha (build 3824). Keep Roland's original files so you can go back.
