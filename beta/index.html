MIDI Conductor 0.7.5 beta 4

A test version for invited testers, on DOOM OS 0.7.4. This page has the whole picture: everything 0.7.5 adds to 0.7.4, so it doesn't matter whether you tried beta 1, 2 or 3. If you did, what's new in beta 4 is just below. Thank you for trying it.

Beta, experimental. Modified firmware can brick your SP-404MKII. This build is tested on one unit only. Back up your SD card and projects first, and keep Roland's original 5.52 files so you can go back. Please don't share this page.

New in beta 4

  • GHOST's and WRAITH's sounds belong to the project. Each project keeps its own starting sound for each synth. PERSIST PATCH CHANGES in the synth's menu saves it for this project; live tweaks you don't persist go when you change project, so the project always opens on its starting sound. AUTO SAVE (SETUP, CTRL 3) saves changes as you make them instead. A new project starts from your old power-up patch if you had one. Copying a project copies its synth sounds.
  • WRAITH's settings that did nothing are new ones: GLIDE in NOISE's place (FX1 page 2; CC 5) slides each note from the one before. HARM EG in DECAY's place (FX2 page 2) moves HARMONICS with the EG. AUX EG in XMOD's place (EG bank page 2) crossfades Plaits' two outputs with the EG. EG VEL (AMP EG page 2) makes soft notes move the EG less.
  • Recorded dial moves: a dial with nothing recorded records one pass of the pattern from your first turn, then plays it back, and you can see the dial move. REC stays on: turn a dial with moves to take it over while you turn it; half a second after you stop it lets go. MARK + REC ends recording. This is the same on MIDI Controls, GHOST and WRAITH.
  • SP PADS for drum sequencers: in a route's TRACKS, a link (turn CTRL 3 past the last track) or OTHER CH can be SP PADS: that channel's notes reach the SP's pads by note number, so kick, snare and hats on one channel each hit their own pad.
  • TRACKS is under the boxes on a route's screen: VALUE goes right along NOTES, CC, BEND and PRES, then down to TRACKS.
  • The SP's VOLUME works again while SENDS, the TRACKS table or a GHOST/WRAITH menu is up.
  • Copying a project on the SP brings its MIDI setup with it (devices, MIDI FX, routes and more), with a MIDI SETUP FROM PROJECT popup. Please tell us if it doesn't.
  • STEPS, LFO and ENV on a WRAITH track offer WRAITH's own settings by name, in its screens' order (MODEL to the LFO's RATE and INT), not 128 numbered CCs.
  • GHOST's and WRAITH's devices have RAW CCS locked on HIDE.
  • A device on ALL channels says CH ALL on the MIDI INSTRUMENT page (it said CH 1E).

Check two SP settings: in UTILITY > SYSTEM > MIDI, Pad Note Out and SEQ Note Out must be ON. MIDI Instruments, GHOST and WRAITH get their notes from the SP's note output, so with either off the pads or the patterns are silent on them.

Everything new since 0.7.4

CIRCLE: chords, keys and progressions

  • CIRCLE, a new MIDI FX: every note becomes a chord from a key, with one chord for the whole project, so a bass, a pad and an arp follow the same change. A change waits for the CHANGE line (a beat, 1, 2 or 4 bars) so it lands in time.
  • Chords from the pads: pads 1 to 7 play the key's chords, pads 9 to 15 their type, pad 16 plays a waiting chord now. A dim green pad shows where the chord likes to go next, a cyan one a softer move.
  • Voicings, inputs and strum: ROOT, inversions, OPEN, WIDE, SMOOTH, SPREAD, QUARTL and DROP2; INPUT ANY, KEYS, KEYS+ (a second key picks the chord's type) or PADS; STRUM up or down.
  • Keys: 14 scales, chord names spelt by the key, and FREE mode: one chord shape (MAJ7, MIN9, MIN7B5 and 15 more) on any of 12 roots. Hold ROLL and press pads 1 to 7 to jump the key: HOME, REL, PAR, IV, V, +1, +2. MARK on the KEY page gives the pattern its own key.
  • Progressions: eight a project (A to H), up to 16 chords each, a half beat to 4 bars per chord. Hold MARK and tap chord pads to record one. A pattern can have its own, and steps can be rests, borrow from the parallel key (PAR) or play their negative (NEG).
  • Negative harmony and HOLD: the SP's own HOLD key holds the chord; hold it and press a chord pad to play the chord's negative.
  • Chord viewer: CIRCLE's last page draws the chord on the circle of fifths, with the next chord beside it.

More MIDI FX

  • STEPS, a step sequencer for any value: up to 16 steps for a synth's CC or a setting of another slot (CIRCLE's chord, a Euclid's HITS, an Echo's DECAY), with GLIDE. Set the steps with the pads.
  • SCALE: SNAP MAP (the white keys play the scale in order), and the WHOLE and GYPSY scales.
  • SHIFT + VALUE: on an effect's screen it moves through the track's slots, FX1 to FX6; on the CHAIN page through the pattern's tracks.
  • Changed: SHIFT + DEL removes an effect. A tap on DEL alone no longer does; on STEPS and progressions it deletes a step.
  • Deleting a MIDI Instrument removes its MIDI FX too.

Synths

  • WRAITH, a second built-in synth: Mutable Instruments Plaits, one voice, with its own programs. GHOST is VCO only again (in beta 1 Plaits was GHOST's oscillator).
  • Both synths are on the DEVICES channel dial, past channel 16: ALL, then GHOST, then WRAITH. Nothing needs waking. ROLL + REVERSE switches the settings between them. A device turned past them and back keeps its own name and CC names.
  • MicroFreak in the patcher's synth list.

Mixer and routing

  • SENDS: MARK on DOOM OS's mixer opens the track's sends to BUS 1, BUS 2 and MASTER. GHOST and WRAITH have theirs too.
  • A MIDI Instrument's LEVEL and PAN on DOOM OS's mixer go to its synth as CC 7 and CC 10.
  • Tracks by channel: a route from a device to the SP has TRACKS, a list of links from a MIDI channel to an SP track. A sequencer like a HAPAX or Pyramid can play a different track on each channel, and one channel can play several tracks at once. A device on ALL in DEVICES takes every channel.

Fixes

  • Recording a keyboard onto a MIDI Instrument, GHOST or WRAITH track keeps every note as played, across the whole MIDI range (it folded them into C2 to C4).
  • GHOST's and WRAITH's bus is set on DOOM OS's mixer only, and is now the right one (DRY played into BUS 1, BUS 1 into BUS 2, BUS 2 into DRY).
  • An effect opened from SENDS goes back to SENDS on one EXIT.
  • An arp no longer keeps playing after you let go when two keys make the same notes (a CIRCLE or a Harmonize before it).
  • With WRAITH on, MIDI FX no longer run at double speed while the pattern is stopped.
  • A note repeated on the next step is no longer cut short by the first one's note-off. The PADS popup stays up for 3 seconds. STEPS without a target no longer draws NO TARGET over its steps.

1. Patch

Drop SP404MKII_APP1.bin from Roland's 5.52 update below: Roland's own file, not a DOOM OS one or an earlier MIDI Conductor. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Enter the beta password first. Your synths' DEVICES.CSV is made on the patcher page as before.

Install it the same way as DOOM OS (DOOM OS manual). SHIFT + REMAIN shows V0.7.5 B4 on the MIDI SETTINGS title bar. To go back, patch 0.7.4 from the patcher.

Coming from beta 1: GHOST no longer has OSC PLAITS, so a GHOST patch set to PLAITS plays its VCOs. For Plaits, put a device on WRAITH.

2. CIRCLE

  1. On a MIDI Instrument, GHOST or WRAITH track, PITCH/SPEED + FX1 and pick CIRCLE. Tap PITCH/SPEED to give it the pads.
  2. Play notes into the track (pattern, pads or a keyboard): they play the chord. Pads 1 to 7 change it.
  3. Turn VALUE through the pages: page 2 is the key (KEY, MODE, CHANGE), page 3 how it plays (PLAYS, INPUT, STRUM).
  4. A progression: on page 5, hold MARK and tap chord pads, one a bar (pad 8 is a rest), then let go. On page 4 set PLAY to ON and press play. Page 7 is the chord viewer.

3. STEPS

  1. Add STEPS to a slot, press RECORD SETTING and pick its target: a CC of the synth, or a setting of another slot.
  2. Tap PITCH/SPEED. Pad n picks step n; CTRL 2 sets its value. DEL empties it.
  3. Page 2 sets RATE, GLIDE and SYNC.

4. WRAITH

  1. SHIFT + REMAIN, open DEVICES, pick a device and turn its channel (CTRL 3) past 16 to WRAITH.
  2. Make a MIDI Instrument on that device in DOOM OS's instrument picker, and play it.
  3. ROLL + REVERSE switches the settings to WRAITH; ROLL + FX1 to FX6 opens its banks. Turn MODEL to hear Plaits' engines. Its menu has LOAD PROGRAM.
  4. Happy with a sound? RECORD SETTING and PERSIST PATCH CHANGES makes it this project's starting sound.

5. Tracks by channel

  1. In DEVICES, turn your sequencer's channel (CTRL 3) past 16 to ALL.
  2. In ROUTES, make a route from it to the SP and open it. Turn VALUE past PRES down to TRACKS and press it.
  3. Press VALUE on + ADD for a link: CTRL 1 sets its channel, CTRL 3 its track. OTHER CH says what channels with no link do (FOLLOW, OFF or SP PADS). For a drum channel, turn the link's CTRL 3 all the way up to SP PADS.

6. What to test

  • Each project's GHOST and WRAITH sounds: PERSIST, change project and back, AUTO SAVE on and off.
  • Recording dial moves and taking them over; WRAITH's GLIDE, HARM EG, AUX EG and EG VEL.
  • SP PADS with a drum sequencer; copying a project on the SP.
  • Recording a keyboard onto a MIDI Instrument across its whole range, and playing it back.
  • GHOST and WRAITH on DRY, BUS 1 and BUS 2 from the mixer, with an effect on each bus; and SENDS on a few tracks.
  • CIRCLE and its progressions with a pattern running; STEPS on a synth's CC and on CIRCLE.
  • TRACKS links if you have a multi-channel sequencer.
  • Anything from 0.7.4 that behaves differently.

When something goes wrong, note what you pressed and what you expected, and email it to beta@loopcartographer.com with a photo or video of the screen if you can. Say which beta you have: the MIDI SETTINGS title bar shows it (V0.7.5 B4).
