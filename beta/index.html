MIDI Conductor 0.7.5 beta

A test version for a few invited testers: CIRCLE, a chord MIDI FX with key changes you can play live, and Plaits as an alternative oscillator for GHOST, in place of its VCOs. Thank you for trying it.

Beta, experimental. Modified firmware can brick your SP-404MKII. This build is tested on one unit only. Back up your SD card and projects first, and keep Roland's original 5.52 files so you can go back. Please don't share this page.

What's new since 0.7.4

  • CIRCLE, a new MIDI FX: the key's seven chords on pads 1 to 7, one chord for the whole project. Every pad with a CIRCLE plays it, and a change waits for the CHANGE line (beat, 1, 2 or 4 bars) so it lands in time.
  • Key jumps: hold ROLL on a CIRCLE screen and pads 1 to 7 jump the key: HOME, REL (relative), PAR (parallel), IV, V, +1 and +2. The screen shows where each one lands.
  • A key per pattern: on CIRCLE's KEY page, tap MARK and the key becomes the pattern's own. When that pattern comes up, CIRCLE moves to it.
  • Plaits as GHOST's oscillator: Mutable Instruments' macro oscillator takes the place of GHOST's VCOs (OSC: VCO or PLAITS), one voice, with its MODEL, HARM, TIMBRE, MORPH and their envelopes on two banks.
  • Scales: SCALE's SNAP MAP (the white keys play the scale in order), and the WHOLE and GYPSY scales.
  • Mixer: a MIDI Instrument's LEVEL and PAN in DOOM OS's mixer go to its synth as CC 7 and CC 10.

1. Patch

Drop SP404MKII_APP1.bin from Roland's 5.52 update below: Roland's own file, not a DOOM OS one or an earlier MIDI Conductor. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Enter the beta password first. Your synths' DEVICES.CSV is made on the patcher page as before.

Install it the same way as DOOM OS (DOOM OS manual). SHIFT + REMAIN shows V0.7.5 on the MIDI SETTINGS title bar. To go back, patch 0.7.4 from the patcher.

2. CIRCLE

  1. On a MIDI Instrument or GHOST track, PITCH/SPEED + FX1 and pick CIRCLE.
  2. Tap PITCH/SPEED to give it the pads. Pads 1 to 7 are the chords (the dim green one is where this chord likes to go, the cyan one a softer move), pad 8 HOLD, pads 9 to 15 the chord's type, pad 16 NOW.
  3. Play notes into the track (pattern, pads or a keyboard) and they play the chord.
  4. Page 2 is the key: KEY, MODE and CHANGE. Page 3 is how it plays: PLAYS, INPUT (ANY or KEYS) and STRUM.
  5. Hold ROLL for the key jumps. MARK on page 2 stores the key in the pattern; tap it again to clear.

3. Plaits

  1. Wake GHOST (ROLL + REVERSE on DOOM OS's page) if it isn't yet.
  2. SHIFT + REMAIN, open DEVICES, open GHOST's menu and set OSC to PLAITS.
  3. ROLL + FX1 and FX2 are now the PLAITS banks. Turn MODEL to hear its engines. GHOST's filter, envelopes and LFO still shape it.

4. What to test

  • CIRCLE with a pattern running: chord changes on the CHANGE line, HOLD, NOW, and the different VOICE settings.
  • Key jumps live, and patterns with their own keys as you switch patterns.
  • Plaits' models, and the CPU meter on GHOST's SETUP while it plays with DOOM OS's own instruments.
  • Anything from 0.7.4 that behaves differently.

When something goes wrong, note what you pressed and what you expected, and send it over with a photo or video of the screen if you can.
