MIDI Conductor 0.7.5 beta 2

The second test version for invited testers. CIRCLE grows progressions, a chord viewer, FREE chords and negative harmony. STEPS is a new step sequencer for any setting. Plaits moves out of GHOST into a synth of its own, WRAITH. A track's effect sends get their own screen, and one MIDI cable can play a different SP track on each channel. Thank you for trying it.

Beta, experimental. Modified firmware can brick your SP-404MKII. This build is tested on one unit only. Back up your SD card and projects first, and keep Roland's original 5.52 files so you can go back. Please don't share this page.

What's new since beta 1

  • CIRCLE progressions: eight a project (A to H), up to 16 chords each, a half beat to 4 bars per chord. Hold MARK on a progression page and tap chord pads to record one. PLAY ON follows it from play, and a pattern can have its own. Steps can be rests, borrow from the parallel key (PAR) or play their negative (NEG).
  • Chord viewer: CIRCLE's last page draws the chord on the circle of fifths, with the next chord's notes beside it.
  • FREE mode: CIRCLE's MODE past the scales. One chord shape (MAJ7, MIN9, MIN7B5 and 15 more) on any of 12 roots, played from pads 1 to 12.
  • Negative harmony: hold the SP's HOLD and press a chord pad to play its negative. A tap on HOLD holds the chord. HOLD is now the SP's own key, so pad 8 is free.
  • INPUT PADS: CIRCLE's chord pads play the chord themselves, at once, without waiting for the CHANGE line.
  • STEPS, a new MIDI FX: up to 16 steps of values for a CC or any setting of another slot (CIRCLE's chord, a Euclid's HITS, an Echo's DECAY), with GLIDE. Set steps with the pads.
  • WRAITH, a second built-in synth: Mutable Instruments Plaits, one voice, with programs of its own. GHOST is back to its VCOs. Both are found on the DEVICES channel dial, past channel 16: GHOST, then WRAITH. No waking needed.
  • SENDS: MARK on DOOM OS's mixer opens the track's sends to BUS 1, BUS 2 and MASTER.
  • Tracks by channel: a route from a device to the SP gets TRACKS, a list of links from a MIDI channel to an SP track. A sequencer like a HAPAX or Pyramid can play a different track on each channel, and one channel can play several tracks at once.
  • MicroFreak in the patcher's synth list.
  • Changed: SHIFT + DEL removes an effect. A tap on DEL alone no longer does: on STEPS and progressions it deletes a step.
  • Fixes: an arp no longer keeps playing after you let go when two held keys make the same notes (a CIRCLE or a Harmonize before it). With WRAITH on, MIDI FX no longer run at double speed while the pattern is stopped. A device turned past GHOST or WRAITH on the channel dial gets its own name and CC names back. A note repeated on the next step is no longer cut short by the first one's note-off. The PADS popup stays up for 3 seconds. STEPS without a target no longer draws NO TARGET over its steps. CIRCLE spells its key by the mode (DB MAJOR, but C# MINOR). CIRCLE's KEY page hint reads MARK PTN KEY.

1. Patch

Drop SP404MKII_APP1.bin from Roland's 5.52 update below: Roland's own file, not a DOOM OS one or an earlier MIDI Conductor. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Enter the beta password first. Your synths' DEVICES.CSV is made on the patcher page as before.

Install it the same way as DOOM OS (DOOM OS manual). SHIFT + REMAIN shows V0.7.5 B2 on the MIDI SETTINGS title bar. To go back, patch 0.7.4 from the patcher.

Coming from beta 1: GHOST no longer has OSC PLAITS, so a GHOST patch set to PLAITS plays its VCOs. For Plaits, put a device on WRAITH (below).

2. CIRCLE progressions

  1. On a MIDI Instrument, GHOST or WRAITH track, PITCH/SPEED + FX1 and pick CIRCLE. Tap PITCH/SPEED to give it the pads.
  2. Turn VALUE to page 5. Hold MARK and tap chord pads 1 to 7, one a bar (pad 8 is a rest). Let go of MARK.
  3. On page 4, set PLAY to ON and press play: the chord follows the progression. Tap MARK there to give it to the pattern you're on.
  4. On pages 5 and 6, pick a step with its pad and change its CHORD, TYPE, LENGTH, VOICE or COLOUR with the dials. DEL deletes the step.
  5. Page 7 is the chord viewer.

3. STEPS

  1. Add STEPS to a slot, press RECORD SETTING and pick its target: a CC of the synth, or a setting of another slot.
  2. Tap PITCH/SPEED. Pad n picks step n; CTRL 2 sets its VALUE. DEL empties it.
  3. Page 2 sets RATE, GLIDE and SYNC (from play, or from the pattern's top).

4. WRAITH

  1. SHIFT + REMAIN, open DEVICES, pick a device and turn its channel (CTRL 3) past 16 to WRAITH.
  2. Make a MIDI Instrument on that device in DOOM OS's instrument picker, and play it.
  3. ROLL + REVERSE switches the settings between GHOST and WRAITH. ROLL + FX1 to FX6 then opens WRAITH's banks; turn MODEL to hear Plaits' engines. Its menu has LOAD PROGRAM.

5. What to test

  • Progressions with a pattern running, rests, PAR and NEG steps, and patterns with their own progression as you switch.
  • STEPS on a synth's CC and on CIRCLE's CHORD or TYPE, with and without GLIDE.
  • WRAITH and GHOST playing together, and the CPU meter on GHOST's SETUP.
  • SENDS on a few tracks, and TRACKS links if you have a multi-channel sequencer.
  • Anything from beta 1 or 0.7.4 that behaves differently.

A full description of every MIDI FX screen and setting is in the MIDI FX reference that came with the invite.

When something goes wrong, note what you pressed and what you expected, and email it to beta@loopcartographer.com with a photo or video of the screen if you can. Say which beta you have: the MIDI SETTINGS title bar shows it (V0.7.5 B2).
